Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HEYYANIXX Learn Arcade ("we", "the site") at heyyanixx-learn.sites.tab.bot handles information. Short version: we don't ask for your name, email or phone number to play, and we don't have user accounts.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (XP, level, streak, badges, best scores, sound setting) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to us. You can delete it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

The site may also save its files in your browser's cache (a "service worker") so the games load faster and work offline.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not sell or share them.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When ads are enabled:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google advertising cookies.

Sharing your score

When you tap "Share", your phone's own share sheet (or WhatsApp) opens with your score and a link. Nothing is posted unless you choose to send it.

Links to other sites

The site links to Instagram, YouTube and other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

The games are made for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are under 13, please use the site with a parent's or guardian's permission.
