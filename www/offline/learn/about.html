About

Khelo. Seekho. Level up.

HEYYANIXX Learn Arcade is a free collection of short (1–3 minute) learning games made for students and beginners — especially first-year BCA / AI students in India who learn on their phones.

Who made it

It's made by Anish (HEYYANIXX), a BCA (Artificial Intelligence) student and Free Fire India content creator. The idea is simple: make the boring parts of studying feel like a game, so you practise a little every day.

The games

  • Type Rush — a typing shooter and ghost race to build typing speed and accuracy.
  • Code Crash — C and Python basics: predict the output, fix the bug, arrange the lines (based on a BCA Semester-1 "Fundamentals of C" syllabus).
  • Prompt Master — a daily AI-prompting puzzle and prompt duels.
  • Quiz Blitz — spin-the-wheel MCQs on AI, computer fundamentals, digital electronics and maths.
  • Edit Sense — swipe cards on video/photo editing terms.
  • FF Brain and FF Quiz Run — fan-made Free Fire tactics quizzes.

Commerce • CA • Sarkari Exam Zone

  • GST Voucher Master — Tally-style practice: pick the right voucher type, split GST into CGST + SGST or IGST, invoice totals, and TallyPrime shortcut keys (checked against Tally Solutions' official help pages).
  • Debit-Credit Swipe — swipe journal entries using the golden rules of accounting, plus a "CA Survival" mode with compound and adjustment entries.
  • Balance Sheet Sorter — sort items into Asset, Liability or Capital.
  • GST & % Sprint — 60-second mental maths (GST, profit & loss, discount, simple and compound interest) with SSC/bank shortcuts. Every answer is calculated in code.
  • Brain Test — reasoning questions (series, odd one out, coding-decoding, directions, analogies) with a fun Brain Score. It is not an IQ test.
  • Sarkari GK Run — a 15-question ladder of static GK (Constitution, polity, geography, history, science). No current affairs. Coins are game points only.

Free, no login

There are no accounts. Your XP, levels, streaks and best scores are saved only on your own device (in your browser's local storage).

Accuracy

Answers were checked carefully (C and Python output questions were compiled and run; accounting entries follow the standard golden rules; maths answers are computed in code; GK uses only well-established static facts). If you spot a mistake, please tell us on the contact page and we'll fix it.
